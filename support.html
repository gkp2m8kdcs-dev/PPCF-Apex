Frequently asked questions

What devices does PPCF Apex support?

PPCF Apex is designed for iPhone running iOS 16 and later. It is not available on iPad or Mac in this release.

Where is my data stored?

All accounts, transactions, transfers, and receipt photos are stored locally inside the iOS app sandbox on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

Do I need an account or sign-up?

No. PPCF Apex has no login, no account, and no subscription. Install it and start journaling.

How do I add a photo receipt to a transaction?

Open a transaction, tap the receipt area, and choose "Take Photo" or "Choose from Library". The first time, iOS will ask for Camera or Photo Library permission. You can decline either and continue using all other features.

Can I use multiple currencies?

Yes. Each account can be denominated in any of twelve supported currencies, and the dashboard converts balances to your selected home currency for an accurate net worth view.

Can I back up my data?

Local data is included in iOS device backups if you have iCloud or iTunes backups enabled. Backups are governed by Apple's privacy and security policies.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the PPCF Apex app from your iPhone. All stored accounts, transactions, and receipt images are removed with it. Because we never transmitted your data, there is nothing to delete on a server.

Is PPCF Apex a brokerage or trading platform?

No. PPCF Apex is a personal finance and investment journal and analyzer. It does not place trades, connect to live data feeds, or recommend securities.

Troubleshooting

The dashboard shows unexpected totals

Check that each account is set to the correct currency and that your home currency matches your expectation. Mixed-currency balances are converted using fixed reference rates bundled with the App — they are intended for journaling, not live valuation.

I accidentally deleted a transaction

PPCF Apex does not maintain a separate cloud backup. If you have iOS device backups enabled, you may be able to restore from a previous backup that contained the entry.

Receipt photo looks blurry

Capture the receipt in good, even lighting and hold the iPhone steady. Tap to focus before capturing. For best OCR-like legibility later, frame the receipt so it fills most of the viewfinder.
