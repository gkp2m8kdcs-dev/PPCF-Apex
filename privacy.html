Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PPCF Apex ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you use it. We have written it in plain English — please read it carefully.

In short: PPCF Apex does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal or financial data. Everything you record stays on your iPhone.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

PPCF Apex is designed from the first launch to keep your data on your device. We do not collect:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number, date of birth).
  • Account credentials of any kind — there is no login.
  • Financial account numbers, balances, transactions, or attachments.
  • Device identifiers, advertising identifiers (IDFA), or fingerprinting signals.
  • Location data, contacts, calendar entries, or other on-device personal content.
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or telemetry.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following data only inside the iOS app sandbox on your iPhone, in JSON files and JPEG attachments:

  • Accounts you create (name, type, currency, balance, notes).
  • Transactions and inter-account transfers you record.
  • Categories, tags, and receipt photos you attach to entries.
  • App preferences such as home currency and disclaimer acknowledgment.

This data never leaves your device unless you choose to export or share it yourself using standard iOS features. We have no server infrastructure to receive it.

3. Permissions Requested by the App

The App requests only the iOS permissions required for its core functionality:

  • Camera — used solely to capture a photo receipt to attach to a transaction. The App does not record video or upload images.
  • Photo Library (read-only) — used solely to attach an existing image as a receipt. The App does not modify or upload your library.

You may decline either permission and continue using the App. If denied, photo receipt features will be unavailable, but all other features remain fully functional.

4. Network and Third-Party Services

PPCF Apex makes no outbound network calls related to your financial data. It does not connect to exchanges, brokers, market data feeds, advertising networks, or analytics platforms.

The App contains no third-party tracking SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no social or sharing SDKs.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. Because no data leaves the device, this requirement is structurally enforced.

6. Data Backup, Export, and Deletion

  • Backup. Local data may be included in iCloud or iTunes backups if you have iOS device backup enabled. These backups are governed by Apple's privacy and security policies, not ours.
  • Export. You may share or export your data through standard iOS share sheets when you choose to.
  • Deletion. Deleting the App removes all locally stored data, including receipt images. We have no copy to delete from any server because no copy was ever made.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the App or applicable law. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent change. Material changes will be highlighted in-app on your next launch.
